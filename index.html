RUSTY Publisher

A personal publishing tool for the animated series RUSTY.

RUSTY is a short animated series created with the help of AI tools and published as vertical videos on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube.

RUSTY Publisher is a small desktop tool used only by the creator of the series. It uploads finished episodes from the creator's own computer to the creator's own social media accounts, so each new episode goes out on all platforms at the same time.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in to each platform with the platform's official login and approves the requested permissions.
  2. The creator selects the finished episode and reviews the title, caption, privacy setting and interaction settings.
  3. After the creator confirms, the tool uploads the video through the platform's official API.

The tool does not read other people's data, does not post comments or likes, and does not access any account other than the creator's own.
