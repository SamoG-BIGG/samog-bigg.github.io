Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how RUSTY Publisher ("the tool") handles information. The tool is operated by Samuel Galajda (Slovakia) and is used only to publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own accounts.

Information the tool accesses

  • Account information provided by the platform after login: account ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube (Google), used only to upload videos and read their publishing status.
  • Video files and captions chosen by the creator for upload.

How information is used

Information is used only to upload the creator's videos, set their captions and privacy settings, and confirm that publishing succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer and are never sent to any server other than the official API of the platform that issued them. The tool has no separate server or database and does not collect analytics.

Sharing

Information is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Videos are sent only to the platform the creator chooses.

Google user data

RUSTY Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features are provided through YouTube API Services; by using them you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the tool's access at any time at Google security settings.

TikTok, Instagram and Facebook data

Data received from TikTok and Meta is handled in line with the TikTok Terms of Service and the Meta Platform Terms. Access can be revoked at any time in the security or app settings of each platform.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the connection is needed and are deleted when an account is disconnected. To request deletion of any data, contact us at the address below; requests are handled within 30 days.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and is not used to collect information from children.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
